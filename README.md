# Prime Home Fixers Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Prime Home Fixers is a Dubai-based home maintenance and repair service provider offering electrical, plumbing, air-conditioning, painting, carpentry, and general property maintenance services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on establishing business credibility, organising maintenance services more clearly, and providing customers with a straightforward enquiry journey. The design targets homeowners, tenants, landlords, and property owners looking for reliable home repair and maintenance solutions in Dubai.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Website Credibility and Business Information
- Addressed placeholder team and project content, including unverified profiles and incomplete statistics.
- Prioritised genuine company information and relevant business photography.
- Improved the presentation of customer feedback and business credibility information without relying on unsupported claims.
- Created a more professional and trustworthy representation of the company.

### 2. Clearer Customer Enquiry Journey
- Improved the presentation of telephone and WhatsApp contact options.
- Introduced a clearer three-step enquiry process covering service requests, quotations, and appointments.
- Made it easier for customers to understand what information to provide when requesting assistance.
- Reduced uncertainty about what happens after submitting an enquiry.

### 3. Better Service Organisation and Clarity
- Organised maintenance services into clearer categories.
- Improved the presentation of electrical, plumbing, AC, painting, carpentry, and general maintenance services.
- Added clearer service descriptions and relevant enquiry pathways.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify the right service for their specific maintenance requirements.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Improved the presentation of company information, service content, and credibility elements.
- **Layout:** Established a clearer hierarchy between the homepage introduction, service categories, trust information, and enquiry sections.
- **User experience:** Simplified service discovery and made the customer enquiry process easier to understand.
- **Consistency:** Improved the organisation of service descriptions, contact options, and business information.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a cleaner and more professional homepage focused on communicating the company's capabilities and encouraging customer enquiries.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses the three critical issues identified during the original website research: weak website credibility, an unclear customer enquiry journey, and poorly organised service information. The overall objective is to present Prime Home Fixers as a professional home maintenance provider while making its services easier to explore and its enquiry process more straightforward.
